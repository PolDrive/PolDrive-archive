# Library artwork

Company logos, console logos and native-alpha console hardware photos are bundled for offline use.
Original authors and brand owners retain their respective rights. Individual asset licenses apply independently of application code.

Hardware images are displayed using their supplied alpha channels. WoloDrive does not remove white pixels or alter hardware colors at runtime.
The 32X and Super Cassette Vision photos have background-extracted variants prepared with OpenAI imagegen from the credited source photos. Other replacement cutouts are sourced as transparent PNGs; upstream preparation is recorded in SOURCES.json.
Company logos retain their original colors. Sony's black wordmark and console logos are rendered in white for contrast while preserving their original alpha. Transparent padding is ignored for consistent visual sizing.

Console logo collection: [HVR88 / Monochrome Gaming Logos](https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos). See its [usage disclaimer](https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos#usage-disclaimer); these marks identify their respective brands and platforms.

Arcade cards use system logos rather than console hardware photos. The Other consoles category uses a text label; Super Cassette Vision uses its hardware photo and name without a separate logo.
